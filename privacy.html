Privacy policy: Hisoblab Publisher

Last updated: 25 October 2026

Hisoblab Publisher is an internal tool of the "Hisoblab ko'ramiz" channel. It is used only by the channel owner to upload the channel's own videos to its own YouTube channel and to read that channel's basic statistics.

What data the app accesses

With the owner's consent, the app uses the YouTube Data API scopes youtube.upload and youtube.readonly for the owner's own channel only. It does not access any other user's account or data.

What we store and share

The app stores one OAuth refresh token on our private server so uploads can run on schedule. We do not collect, sell or share personal data, and we do not use Google user data for advertising. Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
